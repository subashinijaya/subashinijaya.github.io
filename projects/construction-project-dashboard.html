Construction Project Performance Dashboard

Power BI dashboard analysing cost, duration and risk across 1,300 construction tasks — helping teams spot which tasks drive the most cost and risk exposure.

Power BI DAX Power Query

01 Business problem

Project teams need to quickly identify which tasks are high-cost, high-risk or resource-heavy, so management attention goes where it matters most.

02 Background & dataset

Task-level construction dataset covering duration, labor, equipment, material cost and risk classification.

  • 1,300 construction tasks

03 Objectives

  • Track total project cost and task volume at a glance
  • Compare cost and duration across risk levels (Low / Medium / High)
  • Identify which tasks are the biggest cost and risk drivers
  • Support resourcing decisions using labor and equipment data

04 Tools & technologies

Power BIPower QueryDAXPython (pandas)

05 Data cleaning & preparation

Data type validation in Power Query, with initial data validation in Python (pandas) before load.

06 Methodology

  • KPI cards: total tasks, total material cost, average duration, average labor, high-risk task %
  • Cost breakdown by risk level (bar chart)
  • Duration-vs-cost scatter plot, sized by labor requirement, coloured by risk
  • Sortable task-level cost and risk table
  • Interactive slicers for risk level and dependency count

07 Dashboard & visualisations

Overview page — KPI cards and cost breakdown by risk
Cost and risk analysis page — duration vs cost scatter plot

08 Key insights

  • High-risk tasks are isolated quickly through the risk-segmented cost breakdown
  • The duration-vs-cost scatter plot (sized by labor, coloured by risk) surfaces multi-variable outliers a single chart couldn't show
  • Sortable task-level table gives project managers a drill-down path from summary KPIs to individual tasks

09 Challenges & limitations

The dataset is task-level and does not include actual project dates, a start/end timeline, or an on-time-vs-delayed flag. It supports cost, duration and risk analysis, but not calendar-based progress tracking — a future version could add scheduling data for true delay analysis.

10 What I learned

  • DAX measure design for segmented KPI reporting — COUNTROWS and CALCULATE with filter conditions
  • Scatter plot analysis for multi-variable comparison (cost, duration, labor, risk)
  • Structuring a dashboard for stakeholder-ready reporting
