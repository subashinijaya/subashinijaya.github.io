Financial Performance Dashboard

A Power BI dashboard turning 9,800 retail sales orders into executive-ready revenue insights across regions, segments and product categories.

Power BI DAX Power Query

01 Business problem

Decision-makers need a clear, at-a-glance view of revenue performance — which regions, segments and categories are driving sales, and how performance trends over time — rather than piecing figures together manually each reporting cycle.

02 Background & dataset

Built on a retail sales dataset (Superstore) covering order dates, customer segments, regions and product categories.

  • 9,800 sales orders analysed
  • 2015–2018 date range
  • Dimensions: region, segment, category, sub-category

03 Objectives

  • Track total revenue, order volume and average order value at a glance
  • Analyse monthly and yearly revenue trends
  • Compare performance across regions, segments and product categories
  • Identify top revenue-driving products and sub-categories

04 Tools & technologies

Power BIDAXPower Query

05 Data cleaning & preparation

Date parsing and data type validation performed in Power Query to ensure order dates and numeric fields loaded correctly for time-intelligence calculations.

06 Methodology

System built in four layers: data collection (the 9,800-order dataset), data processing (Power Query date parsing and validation), analysis (DAX measures for revenue KPIs and time intelligence), and visualisation (a 2-page Power BI report).

  • KPI cards: total revenue, total orders, average order value, top region
  • Monthly revenue trend line chart with year-over-year comparison
  • Revenue breakdowns by region and segment
  • Category and sub-category revenue ranking
  • Interactive slicers for year, region and segment

07 Dashboard & visualisations

Overview page — revenue KPIs and monthly trend
Category analysis page — revenue ranking by category and sub-category

08 Key insights

  • Revenue, order volume and average order value are visible at a glance through KPI cards, replacing manual reporting
  • Monthly trend view with year-over-year comparison surfaces seasonal patterns across the 2015–2018 period
  • Regional and segment breakdowns identify which parts of the business are driving revenue
  • Category-level ranking highlights the specific products generating the most sales

09 Business recommendations

  • Use the top-region and top-category views to prioritise inventory and marketing focus
  • Extend the model with cost data (see limitation below) to move from revenue tracking to true margin analysis

10 Challenges & limitations

The dataset contains sales revenue only — it does not include cost, profit margin or budget figures, so budget-vs-actual and profitability analysis were out of scope for this version. A future iteration could incorporate cost data for full financial variance analysis.

11 What I learned

  • Time intelligence in Power BI — building monthly/yearly trend aggregations from raw transaction dates
  • DAX measure design for sales KPIs, including DISTINCTCOUNT and AVERAGEX patterns
  • Multi-level category analysis and ranking visuals
