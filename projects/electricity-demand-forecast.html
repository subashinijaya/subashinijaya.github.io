Electricity Demand Forecast Dashboard

Power BI dashboard analysing 25 years of electricity consumption across 10 major economies, with statistical demand forecasting for energy planning.

Power BI DAX Time Series Forecasting

01 Business problem

Energy planning decisions need a clear read on consumption trends and a credible forward projection — not just a historical snapshot — to support capacity and investment decisions.

02 Background & dataset

Historical electricity consumption data for 10 countries — Australia, Brazil, Canada, China, Germany, India, Japan, Russia, UK, USA — covering 2000–2024.

  • 10 countries × 25 years = 250 rows of yearly data

03 Objectives

  • Analyse electricity consumption trends across countries, 2000–2024
  • Identify the highest and lowest consuming countries year over year
  • Forecast electricity demand 5 years ahead using statistical forecasting
  • Compare renewable energy adoption against fossil fuel dependency and carbon emissions

04 Tools & technologies

Power BIPower QueryDAX Python (pandas)Excel/CSV

05 Data cleaning & preparation

Cleaned and deduplicated in Power Query, then aggregated to one consistent row per country per year for time-series analysis.

06 Methodology

  • Multi-country yearly consumption trend line chart
  • Statistical demand forecast — 5-year projection with 95% confidence interval, using Power BI's Analytics forecasting engine (exponential smoothing)
  • Country comparison: consumption ranking, renewable share vs fossil dependency scatter plot
  • Interactive slicers for country and year
  • DAX measures for YoY growth, averages and totals

07 Dashboard & visualisations

Overview page — consumption trends by country
Forecast page — 5-year demand projection with confidence interval
Comparison page — renewable share vs fossil dependency

08 Key insights

  • Clear ranking of highest and lowest electricity-consuming countries across the 25-year window
  • A 5-year statistical forecast with confidence intervals gives planners a range, not a single misleadingly precise number
  • Renewable-share vs fossil-dependency comparison highlights how far each country's energy mix has shifted

09 Challenges & limitations

The dataset is yearly and country-level, not monthly or region-level, and includes no smart grid or sensor-level data. The forecast is a statistical projection based on historical trend patterns available in the data — a demonstration of forecasting methodology, not an official energy planning forecast.

10 What I learned

  • Time-series data cleaning and deduplication
  • Power BI statistical forecasting — exponential smoothing and confidence intervals
  • DAX measure design for YoY growth and aggregations
  • Dashboard storytelling across multiple pages
